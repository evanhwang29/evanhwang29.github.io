My Technical Journey

My name is Evan Hwang, and my first my first experience with technology and actually working with it is taking a robotics class in middle school. In that class, we were taught "JavaScript"
on a website called p5js.org, albeit in hindsight, the skills I learned would probably never be applied elsewhere. However, this would plant a spark in my head, and I was very interested
in technology during that class. That's what lead me to The Knowledge House, where I quickly learned how to make websites with HTML and CSS.

This course has shown me how coding is something I enjoy doing as a hobby, but not really as a career. I plan to use web development as an avenue through which I can express my creativity
such as logging thoughts, artwork, literature I appreciated, etc. If I have the opportunity to take another course on coding, I am unsure if I would take it, as I feel like I learned most
of the basics of coding websites, and most of the things I have yet to learn are more of a niche usecase, of which can largely just be googled and be done with.
